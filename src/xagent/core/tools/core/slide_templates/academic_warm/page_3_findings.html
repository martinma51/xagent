§ FINDINGS
Three findings, in order of force
A short framing sentence that introduces the findings and tells the reader how to weigh them against each other.
i.
The first finding stated as a single, declarative sentence — the kind a reader could quote back without context.
ii.
The second finding, naming the move that surprised us and what it forces the existing reading to give up.
iii.
A third finding (optional). Use it to qualify the first two, or to gesture at what remains open.
